# VoltFlow ERP — UAT Gate

## Customer and billing
- Customer creation and station isolation.
- Meter installation/removal.
- Meter reading and consumption.
- Tariff/slab calculation.
- Invoice issue, adjustment, credit/debit note.
- Invoice void and accounting reversal.

## Collections
- Cash collection.
- Partial/full invoice allocation.
- Collector custody.
- Settlement.
- Payment void and reversal.
- Cash closing.

## Operations
- Generator runtime and generation.
- Feeder reading and customer connection.
- Fuel receipt, issue and adjustment.
- Maintenance work order, parts, labor and expenses.
- Inventory receipt/issue and procurement.

## HR and accounting
- Attendance, leave, overtime.
- Payroll generation, posting and payment.
- Fiscal period control.
- Trial balance and reconciliation.
- Bank reconciliation.

## Integration and security
- Flutter event idempotency.
- Device registration/approval.
- Collector location.
- Cross-station access denial.
- Posted transaction immutability.
- Audit trail.

## Go-live evidence
Every scenario must have an expected result, actual result, evidence reference, and sign-off status.
